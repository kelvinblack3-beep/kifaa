# ADR-003: Regulatory boundaries

Decision ID: ADR-003
Date: 2026-08-29
Status: provisional

Decision
- KIFAA will not act as a PSP, e-money issuer, or deposit-taker without explicit regulatory and contractual authorisation.
- KIFAA will operate under a non-custodial, provider-dependent model for Milestone 1.

Reason
- Risk and legal exposure of handling customer funds.

Consequences
- Any engineering that implies custody or settlement is blocked until an approved ADR and evidence exist.
