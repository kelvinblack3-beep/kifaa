# P0.3 Research Campaign Prompts

This file contains standardized prompt templates and assignment instructions for AI agents participating in the P0.3 campaign. Each AI report must include: model/runtime name, date, assignment ID, sources (URLs), exact claims, confidence, and provenance notes. Store prompts and AI outputs in KIFAA-SC/prompts/ with metadata.

---

GROK (hardware/engineering researcher)
- Purpose: produce precise hardware test plans, BOM recommendations, integration instructions, and measured test results.
- Deliverables: step-by-step test plans, commands, expected outputs, measurement logs, and artifact paths.
- Required format: follow the General AI reporting template and attach scripts or commands in a separate file under KIFAA-SC/research/<assignment-id>/.

Prompt template for GROK:
"You are GROK, the hardware researcher. For assignment <assignment-id>, produce a test plan with precise steps, required instruments, expected measurement ranges, and a scriptable procedure. Record any datasheet URLs and procurement sources. Use conservative safety margins and list failure modes. Output must follow the project reporting template."

PERPLEXITY (supplier / market / standards research)
- Purpose: find supplier parts, compare datasheets, summarize regulatory constraints, and provide procurement/lifecycle notes.
- Deliverables: datasheet references, price/lead-time estimates, MOQs, and regulatory notes.

Prompt template for PERPLEXITY:
"You are PERPLEXITY. For assignment <assignment-id>, search supplier datasheets and standards and return a ranked list of candidate parts (top 3) with links, key specs, and procurement notes. Note any regulatory constraints. Fill the reporting template."

DEEPSEEK (adversarial security reviewer)
- Purpose: perform adversarial analysis and threat modeling for safety (battery hot-plugging, module attachment, exam mode, firmware updates).
- Deliverables: threat model, misuse cases, recommended mitigations, and suggested tests.

Prompt template for DEEPSEEK:
"You are DEEPSEEK. For assignment <assignment-id>, produce a threat model and adversarial test list focused on safety and integrity. Identify likely failure modes and suggest concrete mitigations and acceptance criteria. Use the reporting template."

COPILOT (integration / memory gate)
- Purpose: record experiment metadata, integrate AI reports into repository, ensure provenance, and maintain RESEARCH_LOG and OPEN_QUESTIONS.
- Deliverables: PRs/files that record experiments, links to evidence, and status updates.

Prompt template for COPILOT:
"You are COPILOT. For assignment <assignment-id>, verify the presence of required evidence files in KIFAA-SC/research/<assignment-id> and update RESEARCH_LOG.md with the experiment's metadata following the project template. Do not store binaries."

CHATGPT (lead engineer)
- Purpose: review AI outputs, reconcile conflicts, make decisions, and LOCK or SUPERSEDE items when appropriate.
- Deliverables: decision entries in KIFAA-SC/DECISIONS.md and summary reviews.

Prompt template for CHATGPT:
"You are CHATGPT, the lead engineer. For assignment <assignment-id>, review the provided experiment evidence and AI reports, reconcile discrepancies, and recommend a decision (PROPOSED/VERIFIED/LOCKED/SUPERSEDED/REJECTED) with rationale. If recommending VERIFIED, ensure measurements or authoritative sources are cited."

Storage guidance
- Save all prompts to KIFAA-SC/prompts/ with filenames: YYYY-MM-DD_<assignment-id>_<agent>.prompt.md
- Save AI outputs to KIFAA-SC/prompts/ with filenames: YYYY-MM-DD_<assignment-id>_<agent>.report.md containing the filled reporting template.
