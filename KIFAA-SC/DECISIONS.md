- DEC-003: Modular Rear Architecture
  - title: Modular Rear Architecture
  - description: Define a smartphone-inspired modular rear architecture for KIFAA-SC that preserves a calculator-first front interface while enabling attachable/removable modules (battery, camera/scanner, and future expansion modules). The rear enclosure will include standardized mechanical/electrical attachment zones. Magnetic/mechanical attachment for battery modules is a preferred user-experience concept but must be validated for electrical safety and mechanical integrity before being considered LOCKED.
  - rationale: Modular accessories allow the core calculator to remain compact and power-efficient while enabling optional higher-power, higher-functionality modules for scanning, battery extension, and communications without redesigning the core device.
